# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is
best for the community, and show empathy toward other participants.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information,
and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository security contact.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.
